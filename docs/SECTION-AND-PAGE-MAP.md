# RWM — section and subsection communication map

Snapshot: 31 October 2026. Routes and templates are written; draft, outline, fictional and unverified statuses remain visible. This map describes what each subsection communicates and the actual current content, not a completed curriculum.

## Platform purpose

ROLL WITH ME is a London-first, mobile-first platform for young people aged approximately 13–20. MOVE TOGETHER. BECOME MORE. It connects rights and everyday judgment, learning and possible futures, local participation, and stories with accountability. Young people are participants and co-designers. The experience should offer belonging, useful choices and support without labelling users or neighbourhoods as criminal or dangerous.

### What a rules topic page currently contains

Title and purpose; draft/preparation status; original artwork; existing draft legal text and official sources where matched; exploration questions; optional private reflection; related topics; self/parent-carer and independent help links. Practical suggestions never guarantee safety, excuse violence or imply that appearance removes rights.

### What an activity detail communicates

The activity, provider and verification status; public venue and borough; age/eligibility; date/time; price/free status; accessibility; capacity/booking state; relevant consent requirements; contact/official URL where genuinely verified; review date and safe next steps. Detail routes use `/opportunities/[slug]`; events can use `/events/[slug]` and registration. Area choices are carried through links.

The current eight activity entries are labelled demo and unverified; they cannot be treated as available programmes. Empty/limited filtered feeds are valid. Verified providers, real schedules, prices, capacities and operational arrangements must be supplied and reviewed.

## The Real

Entry: `/the-real`. The question is “What can I learn from someone else’s experience?” The Real remains the working name for the stories section. The focus is context, consequences, turning points and a way forward, without glamorising harm.

### The collection

`/the-real/collection` contains ST01–ST10, each with a reserved page at `/the-real/collection/st01` through `/the-real/collection/st10`. These slots are awaiting the ten genuine stories the owner intends to supply. No actual contributor, testimony, title or outcome is invented. Reserved pages describe story structure, permission/review and a useful next step.

### Themes

`/the-real/themes` and `/the-real/themes/[theme]` group the existing available fictional examples. Themes should be revised around the genuine supplied material; the code does not imply a finished ten-story editorial programme.

### Share your story

`/the-real/share-your-story` offers a general expression of interest through Ask RWM. It is not an evidence upload, release form, public post or promise to publish. Private or traumatic details should not be requested unnecessarily.

### Full story detail

Existing `/stories/[slug]` pages demonstrate full text, content notes, context, reflection and connections to help or activities. Three current fictional examples are labelled. Genuine publication requires storyteller permissions/releases, age-appropriate consent, editorial and safeguarding review, captions/transcripts where relevant and a reviewed non-graphic presentation.

## Help across all four sections

A young person may ask for support for themselves, or a parent/carer may ask on their behalf. The form shows its attached public topic, topic routing, optional borough/age band and safe email preferences. It should explain privacy, operating hours, referral permissions and urgent alternatives. Parent requests never create hidden access to a young person’s account or messages.

`/ask-rwm`, `/support/services`, `/support/urgent` and `/help` provide form/service/urgent routes. Immediate danger in the UK: call 999. Ask RWM is not an emergency channel or 24/7 live chat. No AI automatically answers or resolves high-risk cases. Intake is closed by default. No actual operated email service, notification delivery or completed external referral is connected. Source actions record restricted human triage/referral work only.

## Supporting sections

- The Circle: moderated voice, selected reviewed Q&A and youth advisory participation; no unrestricted youth chat or direct messages.
- Parents: separate resources and a verified-adult premoderated forum; no identifiable children’s disclosures in public posts and no default parental access to youth activity.
- About/Partners/Impact: transparent operator, parent-led governance, youth advisory role, funding and responsible aggregate outcomes; operational roles/funding must be real.
- Surveys: optional feedback and protocol-led studies; immutable live questionnaire versions, restricted responses and exports. Potential evidence collection stays disabled; no claim of court admissibility or representativeness.
- Shop: secondary merchandise concepts and demo cart; live payments, stock, shipping and refunds are unconnected.
- Account: necessary bookings and consent administration; no public youth profiles.
- Admin: restricted editorial, partner/activity, moderation, safeguarding, surveys, area assignments, attendance, media, grants and audit workspaces. Existing structured editors need usability and integration work.
